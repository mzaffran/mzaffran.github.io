Since November 2024, I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Statistics Department of UC Berkeley, working with Ryan Tibshirani.

In June 2024 I completed my PhD in statistics, under the supervision of Aymeric Dieuleveut from CMAP, Ecole Polytechnique, of Julie Josse, from Inria, and of Olivier Féron (director of the FiME laboratory) and Yannig Goude from EDF R&D.

I am interested in predictive uncertainty quantification, especially with distribution-free finite-sample guarantees in a post-hoc setting. During my PhD, I had a particular emphasis in the context of time series or missing features.
